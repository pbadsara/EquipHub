# EquipHub Booking and Cancellation Policy

## 1. Booking Conditions
- Users must select valid start and end dates for equipment rentals.
- Equipment must be available during the selected rental period.
- Booking requests may require approval from the equipment owner.
- Users should review rental details before confirming a booking.

## 2. Cancellation Rules
- Users should be able to request cancellation before the rental begins.
- Cancellation eligibility will depend on the agreed rental policy.
- Equipment owners should be notified when a booking is cancelled.

## 3. Refund Policy
- Refund eligibility will depend on the timing and status of the cancellation.
- Refunds should be processed through the original payment method where supported.
- Any refund deductions must be clearly communicated to the user.

## 4. Booking Approval
- Equipment owners may approve or reject booking requests.
- Users should receive confirmation when their booking status changes.
- Approved bookings should prevent overlapping reservations.
